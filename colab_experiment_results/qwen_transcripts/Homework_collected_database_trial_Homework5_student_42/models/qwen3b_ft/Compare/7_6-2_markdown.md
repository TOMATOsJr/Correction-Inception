Finding voltage

\[
V(A) = L \frac{di}{dt} = 5 \left(80 \cos(2t) \right) = 40 \cos(2t) A + 20
\]

Finding power

\[
P(A) = i(A) \cdot V(A)
\]

\[
P(A) = 4 \sin(2t) \cdot 40 \cos(2t) = 160 \sin(4t) + 20
\]

Finding energy

\[
W(A) = \int P(A) dt
\]

\[
W(A) = \int (160 \sin(4t) + 20) dt
