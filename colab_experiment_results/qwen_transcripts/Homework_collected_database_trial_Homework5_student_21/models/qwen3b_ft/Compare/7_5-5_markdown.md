From \(0\) to \(1 \times 10^{-6}\) s,
\[
i(t) = \frac{1}{0.005\text{ H}} \int_{0}^{t} V(t) \, dt + i(0)
\]
\[
i(1) = \frac{1}{0.005\text{ H}} \int_{0}^{1 \times 10^{-6}} (0.004 + V) \, dt + (-2 \times 10^{-6} \, A)
\]
\[
i(1) = \left(\frac{1}{0.005\text{ H}} \int_{0}^{1 \times 10^{-6}} (0.004 + V(1 \times 10^{-6} - 0)) \right) - 2 \times 10^{-6} \, A = -1.2 \, \mu A
\]

From \(1 \times 10^{-6}\) s to \(3 \times 10^{-6}\) s,
\[
i(t) = \frac{1}{0.005\text{ H}} \int_{1 \times 10^{-6}}^{3 \times 10^{-6}} (-0.001V) \, dt - (1.2 \times 10^{-6} \, A)
\]
\[
i(3) = -0.2 \, t \Big|_{1 \times 10^{-6}}^{3 \times 10^{-6}} - 1.2 \times 10^{-6} \, A = -1.6 \, \mu A
\]

From \(3 \times 10^{-6}\) s,
\[
i(t) = \frac{1}{0.005\text{ H}} \int_{3 \times 10^{-6}}^{\infty} 0V \, dt - 1.6 \times 10^{-6} \, A
\]
\[
i(1) = 0 - 1.6 \times 10^{-6} \, A = -1.6 \, \mu A
\]
