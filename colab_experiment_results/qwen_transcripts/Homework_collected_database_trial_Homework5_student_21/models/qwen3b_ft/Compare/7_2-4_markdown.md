From \( 0 \) to \( 2 \times 10^{-7} \) s,

\[
v(t) = \frac{1}{2 \times 10^{-7}} \int_0^{2 \times 10^{-7}} i(t) \, dt + v(t_0)
\]

\[
= \frac{1}{2 \times 10^{-7}} \left[ \left( 2 \times 10^{-7} \cdot 0 \right) + (-1 \, mV) \right]
\]

\[
= -1 \, mV
\]

From \( 2 \times 10^{-7} \) to \( 3 \times 10^{-7} \) s,

\[
v(t) = \frac{1}{2 \times 10^{-7}} \int_{2 \times 10^{-7}}^{3 \times 10^{-7}} i(t) \, dt + v(t_0)
\]

\[
= \frac{4 \times 10^{-6}}{2 \times 10^{-7}} \left[ 4 \times 10^{-6} + (-1 \times 10^{-2} \, V) \right]
\]

\[
= 0.001 \, V_{ac}
\]

From \( 3 \times 10^{-7} \) to \( 5 \times 10^{-7} \) s,

\[
v(t) = \frac{1}{2 \times 10^{-7}} \int_{3 \times 10^{-7}}^{5 \times 10^{-7}} i(t) \, dt + v(t_0)
\]

\[
= \frac{5 \times 10^{-7}}{2 \times 10^{-7}} \left[ 5 \times 10^{-6} + (0.001 \, V) \right]
\]

\[
= 0.001 \, V
\]

For \( t \geq 5 \times 10^{-7} \),

\[
v(t) = -\frac{1}{2 \times 10^{-7} \, F} \int_{5 \times 10^{-7}}^\infty 0 + (-0.001 \, V)
\]

\[
= 0 + (-0.001 \, V)
\]

\[
v(t \geq 5 \times 10^{-7}) = -0.001 \, V_{ac} = 1 \, mV
\]
