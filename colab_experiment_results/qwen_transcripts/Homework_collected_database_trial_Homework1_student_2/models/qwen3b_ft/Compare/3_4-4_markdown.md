Current divider principle

\[
i_1 = \frac{\frac{1}{10}}{\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{8}} (-6 \text{ A})
\]

\[
i_1 = \frac{\frac{1}{10}}{\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{8}} (-6) = \frac{1}{10} \cdot \frac{16}{3} (-6) = -2 \text{ A}
\]

\[
i_2 = \frac{\frac{1}{8}}{\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{8}} (-6) = \frac{\frac{1}{8}}{\frac{7}{24}} = \frac{1}{2} (-6) = -3 \text{ A}
\]

\[
i_1 + i_2 = i_1 \quad \rightarrow \quad i - 3 \text{ A} = -2 \text{ A} \quad \rightarrow \quad i = 1 \text{ A}
\]
