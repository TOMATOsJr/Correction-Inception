KVL: 
\[
v_s - v_R = 0
\]
\[
15V - v_R = 0 \rightarrow v_R = 15V
\]

\[
p = \frac{v^2}{R} \rightarrow p = \frac{(15)^2}{5} = 45W \textcolor{red}{\text{absorbed}}
\]

\[
p = v \cdot i \rightarrow 45 = 15i \rightarrow i = 3A
\]

\textcolor{red}{(b)} Changing the current source has no impact on the current running through the resistor. This is because \( i = \frac{v}{R} \) and \( v \) comes directly from \( v_s \). Thus, the power does not change, even though \( p = v \cdot i \) and \( v \) and \( i \) are not changing.
