Ohm's Law for \( R_1 \):
\[
\frac{V_{01}}{R_1} = i
\]

Ohm's Law for \( R_2 \):
\[
\frac{V_{02} - V_0}{R_2} = i
\]

Ohm's Law for \( R_3 \):
\[
\frac{V_2 - V_0}{R_3} = i
\]

\[
\Rightarrow V_{01} = \frac{R_1(V_1 - V_2)}{R_2} + V_1
\]
\[
\Rightarrow V_{02} = \frac{R_3(V_1 - V_2)}{R_2}
\]

Since \( V_0 = V_{01} - V_{02} \),
\[
\Rightarrow V_0 = \frac{R_1(V_1 - V_2)}{R_2} + V_1 - \frac{R_3(V_1 - V_2)}{R_2}
\]
\[
\Rightarrow V_0 = \frac{R_1V_1 - R_1V_2 + R_2V_1 - R_3V_1 + R_2V_2 - R_3V_2}{R_2}
\]
\[
\Rightarrow V_0 = \frac{(R_1 + R_2)V_1 - (R_1 + R_3)V_2}{R_2}
\]
