Solving for \( i_1 \):
\[
\text{Parallel resistance} = \frac{1}{\frac{1}{16} + \frac{1}{8}} = \frac{16}{3}
\]
\[
i_1 = (-6) \cdot \left( \frac{16}{3} \right) = -\frac{2}{3} \, \text{A}
\]

Solving for \( i_2 \):
\[
\text{Parallel resistance} = \frac{1}{\frac{1}{8} + \frac{1}{8}} = 4 \, \Omega
\]
\[
i_2 = (6) \cdot \left( \frac{4}{8} \right) \Rightarrow i_2 = -3 \, \text{A}
\]

KCL at center node:
\[
i_1 = i + i_2
\]
\[
-\frac{2}{3} = -3 + i
\]
\[
i = 1 \, \text{A}
\]
