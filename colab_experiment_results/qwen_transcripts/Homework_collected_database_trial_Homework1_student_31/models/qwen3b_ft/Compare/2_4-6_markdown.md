 The circuit element with resistance of \( 4 \Omega \) does not adhere to passive convention and the circuit element with resistance of \( 3 \Omega \) adheres to passive convention. Therefore:

\[
V_1 = -R_1 \cdot i_1 = -4 \cdot 2 = \textcolor{red}{-8 \text{ V}}
\]
\[
V_2 = R_2 \cdot i_2 = 8 \cdot 2 = 16 \text{ V}
\]

Power absorbed by \( R_1 = -V_1 \cdot i_1 = -(-8) \cdot 2 = 16 \text{ W} \)
Power absorbed by \( R_2 = V_2 \cdot i_2 = 16 \cdot 2 = 32 \text{ W} \)
