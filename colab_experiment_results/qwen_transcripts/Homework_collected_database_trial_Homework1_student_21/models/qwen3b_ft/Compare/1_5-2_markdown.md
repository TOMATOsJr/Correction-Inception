a) Find the charge in coulombs that passes through the range

\[
i = \frac{dr}{dt} = 10 \textcolor{red}{\frac{d}{dt}} \quad \text{(for 2 hrs = 7200 sec)}
\]

\[
q = \int_{-\infty}^{t} i dt = \int_{0}^{7200} 10 dt + q(0)
\]

\[
= 10t + q(0) \Bigg|_{7200}
\]

\[
= [10(7200)] + q(0) - [10(0) + q(0)]
\]

\[
= 72000 + q(0) - 0 - q(0)
\]

\[
q = 72000 \text{ C}
\]

b) Find the power absorbed by the range

\[
p = v \cdot i
\]

\[
p = (110 \text{ V}) \cdot (10 \text{ A})
\]

\[
= 1100 \text{ W}
\]

c) If electric energy costs 12 cents per kilowatt-hour, determine the cost of operating the range for 2 hours

\[
\text{cost} = p \cdot 12 \text{ hr}
\]

\[
= (1.1 \text{ kW} \cdot 12 \text{ hr}) \cdot 2
\]

\[
= \text{Rs. } 26.40
\]
