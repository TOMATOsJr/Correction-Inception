a) Calculate the current \( i \) in the resistor and the power absorbed by the resistor.

\[
v = iR
\]
\[
i = \frac{v}{R} = \frac{15V}{5\Omega} = 3A
\]
\[
p = v \cdot i = (15V)(3A) = 45W
\]
