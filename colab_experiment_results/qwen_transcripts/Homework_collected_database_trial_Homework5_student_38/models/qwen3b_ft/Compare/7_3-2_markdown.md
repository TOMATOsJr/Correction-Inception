For capacitor charged at \( t = -\infty \):
\[
W_c(t) = \frac{1}{2} (v^2(t)) = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 10^{-5} \cdot (5 - 5e^{-4000t})^2
\]
\[
= \frac{1}{9200} - \frac{1}{4000} e^{-4000t} + \frac{1}{8000} e^{-8000t}
\]

So:
\[
i_c(0) = \frac{4}{5} \text{ A}, \quad W_c(0) = 0 \text{ J}
\]
