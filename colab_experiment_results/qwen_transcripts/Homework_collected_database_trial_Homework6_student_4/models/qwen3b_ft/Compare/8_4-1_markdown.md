 Before \( t = 0 \): (switch open)

\[
V(0^-) = 10 \textcolor{red}{V}
\]

After switch closed:

\[
V_{oc} = 10 \textcolor{red}{V} \cdot \left(\frac{8 \Omega}{16 \Omega}\right) = 5 \textcolor{red}{V}
\]

\[
T = (0.5F \times 4 \Omega) = 0.2 \textcolor{red}{s}
\]

\[
R_T = \frac{1}{\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{8}} = 4 \Omega \quad (\text{when switch closed})
\]

\[
v(t) = 5 + 5 e^{-\frac{t}{T}} \quad V, \quad 0 \leq t < 1.5 \textcolor{red}{s}
\]

\[
v(1.5 \textcolor{red}{s}) = 5 + 5 e^{-\frac{1.5}{T}} = 5.003 \textcolor{red}{V}
\]

After switch open long time:

\[
V_{oc} = 10 \textcolor{red}{V}, \quad R_T = 8 \Omega, \quad T = 0.5F \cdot (8 \Omega) = 0.4 \textcolor{red}{s}
\]

\[
v(t) = 10 - 4.997 e^{-\frac{2.5}{T}(t - 1.5)}, \quad 1.5 \textcolor{red}{s} \leq t
\]
