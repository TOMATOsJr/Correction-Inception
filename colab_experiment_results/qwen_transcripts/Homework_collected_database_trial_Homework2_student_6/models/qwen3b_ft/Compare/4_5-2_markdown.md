Mesh 1:

\[
\frac{i_1 - i_2}{8 \Omega} + \frac{i_3 - i_1}{10 \Omega} + V_1 = 0
\]

\[
V_1 = \frac{(i_1 - i_2)}{8 \Omega} \cdot \frac{(i_3 - i_1)}{10 \Omega}
\]

\[
= \frac{(1 - 2)}{8} \cdot \frac{(1 - 1)}{10} = \frac{\textcolor{red}{(1)}}{8} \cdot \frac{\textcolor{red}{(1)}}{10} = \frac{\textcolor{red}{-10}}{80} = \frac{\textcolor{red}{-26}}{80} = \frac{\textcolor{red}{-13}}{40} = V_C
\]

Mesh 2:

\[
-V_1 + \frac{i_2 - i_1}{4 \Omega} + \frac{i_3 - i_4}{8 \Omega} = 0
\]

\[
V_1 = \frac{i_2 - i_1}{4 \Omega} \cdot \frac{i_3 - i_4}{8 \Omega}
\]

\[
= \frac{(1) - (2)}{4} + \frac{(1) - (4)}{8} = \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{8} = \frac{2}{8} = \frac{1}{4} = V_1
\]
