KVL

\( 12 \textcolor{red}{V} + 24 \textcolor{red}{V} + V = 0 \)

\( V = -36 \textcolor{red}{V} \)

KCL @ \( V_o \) "out"

\( -0.6 + \left( \frac{24 \textcolor{red}{V}}{40 \Omega} \right) = 0 \)

\( i_o = \frac{24 \textcolor{red}{V}}{40 \Omega} = 0.6 \textcolor{red}{A} \)

Power supplied by each source:

- Power of 24V source:
  \( 24 \textcolor{red}{V} \cdot i_o = 24 \cdot 0.6 = 14.4 \textcolor{red}{W} \)

- Power of 0.6A source:
  \( V \cdot 0.6 \textcolor{red}{A} = -36 \cdot 0.6 = -21.6 \textcolor{red}{W} \)

- Power of 12V source:
  \( 12 \textcolor{red}{V} \cdot i_o = 12 \cdot 0.6 = 7.2 \textcolor{red}{W} \)
