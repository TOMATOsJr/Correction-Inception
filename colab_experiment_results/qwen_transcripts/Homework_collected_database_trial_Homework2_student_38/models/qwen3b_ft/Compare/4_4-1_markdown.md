 Current into \( L \): 
\[
i = \frac{V}{12}, \quad i_1 = \frac{Va - Vc}{1} = -\frac{4}{3} A, \quad i_2 = \frac{Vb - Vc}{2} = -4 A, \quad i_1 + i_2 = -\frac{16}{3} A
\]

Current out of ground: 
\[
-\frac{Vd}{2} - \frac{Vb}{2} = -\frac{28}{3} A, \quad -\frac{28}{3} - \frac{16}{3} = -4 A
\]

\[
-4 = A \cdot i_1 \quad \Rightarrow \quad -4 = A \cdot -\frac{4}{3} \quad \Rightarrow \quad A = 3
\]
